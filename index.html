A reader’s guide to data.

What is being counted?

Look for the unit first. A count of households with a water connection answers a different question from the share of people with access to water.

What is the comparison?

Two districts can record the same number of votes and have very different turnout. Divide votes cast by registered electors to compare participation.

How much did it change?

If a share rises from 40% to 50%, it gains 10 percentage points. Relative to the starting share, that is a 25% increase.
