About me

Debodeep Banerjee

I created DesiData to explore India through maps. The questions range from everyday curiosities, like where people searched for Ghibli, to how internet access varies across rural districts.

My research focuses on how AI can help people make decisions. Here, I work with maps to give readers room to explore the evidence and draw their own conclusions.
