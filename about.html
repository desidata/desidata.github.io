About me

Debodeep Banerjee

I created DesiData to explore India through maps. The questions range from everyday curiosities, like where people searched for Ghibli, to how internet access varies across rural districts.

I am an applied AI researcher with particular interest in human-AI collaboration. Here, I work with maps to give readers room to explore the evidence and draw their own conclusions.
